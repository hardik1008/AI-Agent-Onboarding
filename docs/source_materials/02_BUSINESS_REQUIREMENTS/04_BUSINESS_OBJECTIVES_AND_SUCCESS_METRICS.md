# 04. BUSINESS OBJECTIVES & TARGET SUCCESS METRICS

---

## 1. Primary Strategic Objectives
1. **Compress End-to-End Certification TAT**: Reduce median turnaround from **8.4 days to < 3 days** (and < 10 minutes for document review stage).
2. **Eliminate Document Review Queue**: Replace the 17.3-hour queue with instant real-time autonomous document reasoning.
3. **Maximise Straight-Through Processing (STP)**: Autonomously approve clean applications with zero human touch.
4. **Halt Sequential Rework Leakage**: Replace multi-round sequential rework with a single consolidated notification, recovering at least 100+ lost agents annually.
5. **Guarantee Regulatory Compliance**: Achieve ≥ 98% recall on ineligible candidates, preventing compliance violations.

---

## 3. Projected Economic & Business Impact (Simulation Model)
* **Agent Retention Yield**:
  Recovering 50% of the 211 non-responders and nudging 50% of the 482 post-KYC dropouts yields **+245 additional certified POSP agents** annually.
* **Premium Expansion**:
  At an average POSP production rate of ₹2.5 Lakhs GWP in their first year, 245 additional agents generate **₹6.1 Crore in incremental Gross Written Premium**.
* **Operational Cost Savings**:
  Reducing reviewer touch time by 80% saves **₹11.8 Lakhs annually** in direct operational loaded wages, freeing the 22-person team to focus on high-touch partner distribution.
