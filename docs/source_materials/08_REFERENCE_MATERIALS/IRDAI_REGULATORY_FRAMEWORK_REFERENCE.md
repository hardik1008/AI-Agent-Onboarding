# IRDAI Regulatory Framework Reference for POSP Onboarding

> **Statutory Compendium of Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India Mandates**  
> **Key Regulations:** IRDAI/INT/GDL/ECM/044/03/2017 & Insurance Brokers Regulations 2018  
> **Compliance Context:** Point of Sales Person (POSP) Certification & Life/General Insurance Distribution

---

## 1. Statutory Definition & Scope of POSP

Under IRDAI Guidelines (Circular IRDAI/INT/GDL/ECM/044/03/2017), a **Point of Sales Person (POSP)** is defined as:
> *"An individual who solicits and markets pre-underwritten, simplified insurance products approved by the Authority, acting on behalf of an insurer or an insurance intermediary."*

### 1.1 Permitted Insurance Products
POSPs are strictly authorized to distribute pre-underwritten products where coverage, premium tables, and underwriting rules are fully automated and do not require individual underwriter discretion:
- **Motor Insurance:** Mandatory Third-Party (TP) liability and comprehensive packages for private cars, two-wheelers, and commercial vehicles.
- **Health Insurance:** Fixed-benefit health policies, critical illness plans, and individual/family indemnity covers up to regulatory sub-limits (typically sum insured up to ₹5 Lakhs).
- **Personal Accident Insurance:** Standard personal accident and accidental death/disability policies.
- **Travel Insurance:** Domestic and overseas travel insurance covers.
- **Life Insurance:** Pure term insurance policies without medical examination requirement, immediate annuities, and simplified non-linked endowment products.

## 2. Mandatory Eligibility Standards

### 2.1 Minimum Age (§4.a)
- The candidate must have completed **eighteen (18) years of age** on the date of application.
- *Statutory Evidence:* Date of birth recorded on Permanent Account Number (PAN) card, Aadhaar card, or Birth Certificate.

### 2.2 Minimum Educational Qualification (§4.b)
- The candidate must have passed at least the **10th standard (Secondary School Examination)** or equivalent examination from a recognized State Board, Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations (CISCE), or National Institute of Open Schooling (NIOS).
- *Statutory Evidence:* Class 10 marksheet or pass certificate displaying candidate name, father's name, date of birth, and `PASS` outcome.

### 2.3 Identity & Tax Integrity
- Valid individual PAN card issued by the Income Tax Department (4th character `'P'`).
- Linking with Aadhaar under PMLA guidelines.

## 3. Mandatory Training & Certification Mandates

### 3.1 15-Hour Mandatory Training Curriculum (§5)
- Every aspiring POSP must undergo a mandatory **15 hours of structured training** conducted by the sponsoring insurer or insurance broker.
- The curriculum must comprehensively cover:
  1. Fundamentals of Insurance & Principles of Utmost Good Faith (*Uberrimae Fidei*).
  2. Structure and Types of General and Life Insurance Contracts.
  3. Pre-underwritten POSP product features, exclusions, and limits.
  4. Proposal form filling, premium calculation, and digital issuance.
  5. Claims reporting and grievance redressal mechanisms.
  6. Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA) and Anti-Fraud standards.
- *Compliance Requirement:* The platform LMS must maintain auditable time logs verifying that the applicant spent at least 15.0 active hours completing the curriculum before being admitted to the certification exam.

### 3.2 Examination & Certification (§6)
- The candidate must appear for and pass a proctored examination administered by the sponsor in accordance with IRDAI guidelines.
- Successful candidates are awarded a digital **POSP Certificate of Registration** bearing a unique registration number, sponsor broker details, and validity terms.
- A 24-hour mandatory waiting period is required before any re-examination attempt in the event of an initial examination failure.

## 4. The Exclusivity Register & Central De-Registration Rules

Under Section 7 of the POSP Guidelines:
1. **Single-Sponsor Exclusivity:** A POSP can be sponsored by and registered with only **one** general insurer/broker and **one** life insurer/broker at any given point in time.
2. **Central Registry Verification:** Prior to onboarding, the sponsoring intermediary must query the central POSP registry maintained by the Insurance Information Bureau of India (IIB) using the candidate's PAN number.
3. **De-Registration Protocol:** If a candidate is already registered with another entity:
   - The application must be rejected under code `ALREADY_REGISTERED`.
   - The candidate must obtain a formal **No Objection Certificate (NOC)** or surrender certificate from the existing sponsor.
   - The previous sponsor must remove the POSP from the central IIB register before PolicyNest can legally complete certification.

## 5. Intermediary Liabilities & Compliance Audits

Under the IRDAI (Insurance Brokers) Regulations, 2018:
1. **Principal-Agent Liability:** PolicyNest Insurance Brokers is vicariously liable for all acts of solicitation, misrepresentation, or misconduct committed by its certified POSPs.
2. **Record Retention:** All KYC records, proof of education, bank details, training logs, exam scores, and issuance timestamps must be maintained in an immutable, auditable archive for a minimum of **five (5) years** from the date of certification or termination.
3. **Statutory Penalties:** Sponsoring ineligible candidates (e.g., underage or failing Class 10 education), failing to maintain training records, or distributing non-POS products through POSP channels attracts statutory sanctions, fines, and potential cancellation of the broker license under IRDAI inspection.
